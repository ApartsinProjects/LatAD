# Reviewer-coverage audit: does IoT2.html actually answer every Round-1 concern, and does the letter match the paper?

Read-only audit, 2026-09-18. Inputs: `review_round_1/Review1.txt`, `Review2.txt`, `Editor review.txt`, `IoT2_review_response_plan.html`, `IoT2_editor_plan.html`, `response_letter.md` (22:15 on 09-17), and `poc/paper/IoT2.html` (07:35 on 09-18). ### 2.2 R1-2 / Ed-near: the nearest-component likelihood (most exposed item)

Facts on disk:
- `e2_nearest.json` (seed 0, 3 benchmarks + SKAB): difficult AUROC mixture / nearest / tempered = WADI 0.661 / 0.663 / 0.663; HAI 0.807 / 0.808 / 0.807; SWaT 0.909 / 0.904 / 0.906. Rare-regime (pi < 2%) FPR: HAI 0.355 / 0.352 / 0.353; WADI and SWaT 0.0 for all three.
- `e2_rare_summary.json` (3 seeds, ~50 variants per dataset): the mixture-vs-nearest rare-FPR gap is <= 0.005 in every base and shrink variant on HAI, 0.0 on WADI and SWaT.
- `fable_imbalance_design.md`: the induced-imbalance sweep was designed and smoke-tested on SKAB with the recorded expectation "NULL against AE ... LatAD carries no rarity-immune head; under starvation it is an absolute-density detector like the rest", and "the pi-only counterfactual in the sweep gave an ideal-case gap of exactly 0".

So the reviewer's exact experiment (mixture NLL vs nearest NLL vs an imbalance-aware variant, with rare-regime FPR) was run and shows parity; the property "rare-regime-safe" is by construction, not demonstrated, and on these fitted models the choice makes no difference because VaDE never allocates a low-pi component to a rare regime.

What the paper currently says: Table 1 A5 "Score against the nearest component, never the pi-weighted mixture, so a rare-but-valid regime is not penalized"; §4.3(ii) "keeps a rare-regime signal that the mixture sum washes out"; Table 6, where the nearest head is the weakest latent head and lowers the base on two of three datasets. A reviewer will conclude the head is unsupported and possibly harmful, and will note that the letter's "controlled imbalance sweep" is nowhere in the paper.

Fix (no new compute; ~20-30 min of agent time to tabulate and edit):
1. Add the parity result to the paper: either three rows appended to Table 6 (pi-weighted VaDE mixture NLL; nearest-component NLL; pi-tempered), difficult AUROC and rare-regime FPR at pi < 2%, three seeds, or a small Table 6b. State the invariant: at these occupancies the three scores agree within seed noise.
2. Rewrite A5's claim from "demonstrated" to "by construction, and free": "the nearest-component score cannot penalize a valid window for its regime's low prior; on the three benchmarks it is empirically indistinguishable from the mixture score (Table 6b), so the choice costs nothing; its benefit would require a regime imbalance these benchmarks do not exhibit."
3. Fix the Table 6 caption: "latent density" is the 80-component KDE of §4.3(i), not the VaDE mixture; say why base (density + nearest) is below density alone on WADI and SWaT, or the reviewer will ask why the head is kept.
4. Letter R1-2 and E2: replace "We therefore support it with a controlled imbalance sweep" with the parity result and one sentence that the induced-imbalance experiment was designed and found uninformative (every detector flat on the starved regime, HAI `e2_fable_sweep`), and fix "Table 5" to "Table 6".
