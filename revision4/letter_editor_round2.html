Cover Letter to the Academic Editor (Round 2)

Manuscript IoT-4522779, MDPI IoT
Title: Modeling Normal Is All You Need: Joint Latent Clustering for Anomaly Detection in Multimodal Industrial-IoT Cyber-Physical Systems
Authors: Alexander Apartsin and Yehudit Aperstein

Dear Academic Editor,

Thank you for handling our manuscript. We submit the revised version in response to the second round of review. The reviewer raised five points; we address each with either a manuscript change or a reasoned response, set out in full in the accompanying point-by-point letter. This round's changes are highlighted in blue in the revised manuscript.

In brief:

  • We added a direct check that the correlation communities correspond to the plants' documented process stages, showing alignment well above chance (new Appendix C and Table C2).
  • We clarified that the community and whole-plant scores are standardized before they are combined, and made the single-configuration fusion trade-off explicit (§4.4, Appendix A).
  • We stated explicitly that the difficulty rule is detector-agnostic and that the WADI difficult subset is small and underpowered, so the WADI result is reported as a tie rather than a performance claim (§6).
  • We narrowed the empirical conclusion about reconstruction-based detectors to the evaluated methods (USAD, TranAD, GDN), presenting the two recently introduced methods as future-work comparisons (§2.4, §8).
  • On the recommended references, we followed the journal's guideline to include reviewer-recommended references only where they enhance the manuscript; having analyzed the two suggested connectivity-layer studies, we found no direct connection to the paper's topic and did not include them.

We believe the revision addresses the round-2 comments, and we thank you and the reviewer for the careful reading.

Sincerely,
Alexander Apartsin and Yehudit Aperstein
